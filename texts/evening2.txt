It is late evening. Oil lamps light the street outside, and Kevin watches people hurry back home. These people look tired, yet the desire to rest in soft beds seems to give them strength.
Snow starts falling, and white cold particles begin to cover the window. Soon enough, Kevin sees just blurry figures. Then these blurry figures disappear. Not under a clump of snow - they have finally reached their homes, safely.
Kevin gets off the chair, opens the cabinets. After rummaging through the dishes, he eventually finds a cup.
Kevin takes his first step - and almost trips over, before regaining footing.
He makes another step - this time it lands better. Almost natural.
Third step gets even easier.
Step, step, step.
Kevin takes out the coffee jar - tight lid won't budge at first - and takes exactly one and a half spoons worth of coffee.
Hot, steaming water splashes against the cup's bottom, making a few puddles on the counter. Gentle scent of coffee fills the room, creating simple joy for Kevin to remember later.
Snow keeps falling outside, and somewhere in the middle of the night oil lamps die out, leaving just the moon's tender blue light to glow through Kevin's window.
"What a night!" Kevin wonders.
The same night, as always. Kevin rests his old bones down, closes his eyes and falls asleep.
