Sun begins to set. It's getting cold, and Mary puts on her leather jacket. The road back to her hometown goes downhill, and she can see all the houses.
She walks for about an hour, sun hides behind the trees, long shadows fully cover the grassy land. Starry blue black sky shines, reflecting in the seawater.
Mary keeps walking, and the final pink shine of afterglow disappears. Night has fallen.

It's dark on the road, but Mary does see the town - golden light glows from the windows, streetlights, cars, reflecting off white walls and red roofs.
She's not alone - a bustling city keeps her company even if not many people even know her.

"Heeeey, Mary!" a deep male voice echoes through the hills. Mary looks around, squinting her eyes.
A flashlight from his flip phone blinds Mary.
"Argh, you jerk!" Mary says, rubbing her eyes.
He turns light away.
"Yeah, glad to see you too." he says.
"Shouldn't you be on the festival?" Mary opens her eyes slightly, still squinting.
Young man sits on the big rock beside the road.
"You know I'm no party beast." He says.
"Pretty much sure they won't miss big ol' Jason." he snickers.
"Pretty much sure your whole presence makes up that party." Mary mocks Jason.
"Awww, since when are you so worried about them party?" Jason taunts.
Mary doesn't answer him. A deafening silence hangs between her and Jason.
"Wanted to venture a bit. And you?" Jason finally breaks the pause.
"Same." Mary answers curtly.
Jason makes a gesture, inviting Mary to sit on the rock with him. Mary looks at her own phone. Digital clock shows numbers "22" and "17".
Mary hesitates for a moment, her aching legs beg her to settle down. With a huff, she accepts the invitation, carefully lowering her body on the hard surface.
Strangely, it's warm - Jason must've moved from here to make room for Mary.
"You know, I could make few more steps." Mary says.
Jason smiles crookedly, then changes topic: "It's pretty in here."
"Duh. That's why I like it here." Mary states this as the most obvious thing in the world.

Jason and Mary sit on the rock for a while. Leaves rustle in the background with cold yet gentle wind playing with them.
Fireflies dance silently under old oak's branches not so far away from the couple. Mary looks at these little insects.
As her gaze moves to the town, she notices details too small to be seen before: colorful lights, garlands, bunting stand out on otherwise white-golden walls.
Large crowds have filled the streets. Not densely, not hustling - just right amount of people.

Jason gets up and offers Mary a hand. "Shall we return?" Jason's crooked smile turns into stupid grin.
"Don't show anyone your dumb mug." Mary retorts, slightly taken aback with such chivalry. She takes Jason's hand though.
"Wanna keep it all to yourself? Sorry, darling, this face is in public domain!" Jason's grin widens.
Mary releases his hand, all red.
"Stupid! Dumb! Annoying!" Mary declares furiously.
Jason laughs. Soon enough they'll arrive home. Until then, Mary will be enduring merciless patronage.
